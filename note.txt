The main idea of the game:
1. the player opens the website
2. The game shows the pop-up with the name of the game and tap to start
3. The player taps on start
4. The counter starts from 30 sec to 0 sec
5. Player can click on flies and each fly the long tongue touches disappears (I'm not sure how to do this one..)
6. Player gets 1 point for each fly. 
7. When you eat one fly, more flies appear
8. When you reach a certain score the flies fly faster.
9. After 30 sec there is a message with your score and the best score.
